Funding landscape

Grants your business
may already qualify for

Pennsylvania and the federal government fund billions in sustainability upgrades each year. Most small business owners never apply because they don't know these programs exist.

PA DEP — State Grant
Small Business Advantage Grant
$12,000
Reimburses 50–80% of project costs for energy efficiency or pollution reduction. Lighting, HVAC, refrigeration, and more qualify.
PA DEP — State Grant
Alternative Fuels Incentive Grant
$500,000
For businesses transitioning to alternative fuel vehicles or adding EV charging infrastructure. ~$5M available annually.
PA DEP — Low-Interest Loan
Pollution Prevention Assistance Loan
$100,000
2% fixed rate, up to 10-year term. Covers 75% of project costs. Stack with the SBAG grant to maximize coverage.
Federal — Tax Credit
Clean Electricity Credit (Sec. 45Y)
~30%
Federal tax credit for solar, wind, and geothermal energy systems. Solar projects remain eligible through 2027.
